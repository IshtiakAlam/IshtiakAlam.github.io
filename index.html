About

I’m Ishtiak, a data engineering lead based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. For 12 years I’ve built the systems companies use to count money, customers and risk.

I started as a data warehouse and BI developer at IQVIA, building ETL and dashboards for pharma clients. At Banglalink, a telecom operator with 32 million subscribers, I ran the BI team and caught fraud that was costing about USD 190K a month. At Optimizely I architected the Snowflake warehouse that gave the company one answer for ARR and churn.

Today I Data and Analytics Engineering at Axi, a global FX and CFD broker regulated in five jurisdictions. I own the trading, payments, customer, marketing and finance domains on Databricks, dbt and Azure: 45 production jobs, 80+ dbt models, a semantic layer used by 120+ people, and the regulatory reporting that has to be right every day.

I’ve led teams of 4 to 10, mentored 3 engineers and 15+ analysts, and written the standards other people build on. The work I like best is getting a room to agree on what a number means, then making sure it never drifts.
